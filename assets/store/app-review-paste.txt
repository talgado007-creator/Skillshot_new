3. WHAT THE APP DOES AND WHO IT IS FOR
Skillshot is a single-player arcade dodge game. The player moves one circle with a
floating on-screen joystick and survives as long as possible while five kinds of
hazard fly in from the screen edges: fast projectiles, ground area attacks, fixed
beams, homing orbs and returning boomerangs. There are no levels and no waves; the
spawn rate climbs continuously with elapsed time, and the score is simply how long
the player lasted.

It is for casual and arcade players aged 13 and over. It is rated 4+: there is no
violence, no realistic imagery and no communication between users - every visual is
an abstract neon shape drawn in code. A run lasts from seconds to a couple of
minutes, and best times are kept on the device so the player competes with
themselves.

4. HOW TO ACCESS EVERY FEATURE
No account, login, password or sample file is required. Nothing is gated or
purchasable.
- Launch the app. iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt on first launch;
  either answer is accepted and gameplay is identical afterwards.
- The menu has a Loadout panel with three optional abilities (Flash, Dash, Ghost).
  Tap the cards to arm or disarm them. Running with none armed earns a purist mark.
- Tap PLAY. A 3-2-1 countdown runs, then the game begins.
- Drag anywhere to move; a joystick appears wherever the finger lands.
- Tap the round F, D and G buttons to use an armed ability. Each shows its cooldown.
- Touching any hazard ends the run.
- On the death screen: RETRY starts another run, MENU returns to the menu, and
  REVIVE - WATCH AD offers a rewarded advert to continue the same run.
- Customise controls, on the menu, lets the ability buttons be dragged anywhere and
  resized.
- Best times appear on the menu and the death screen, are stored only on the device,
  and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES AND PLATFORMS
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for advertising: interstitial, rewarded video
  and rewarded interstitial.
- Google User Messaging Platform for advertising consent in the EEA and UK.
- Apple App Tracking Transparency for the system tracking prompt.
- Capacitor (Ionic) as the native wrapper; the game itself is HTML5 and Canvas.
There are no servers operated by us, and no authentication service, payment
processor, analytics platform or AI service. No data reaches any service we control.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Gameplay, features and content are identical everywhere. The only difference is
legal: users in the EEA and UK see Google's consent form before personalised adverts
are served, and a Privacy and ad settings button on the menu lets them change that
choice at any time. Elsewhere the form is not shown.
