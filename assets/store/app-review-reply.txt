3. WHAT THE APP DOES AND WHO IT IS FOR
--------------------------------------
Skillshot is a single-player arcade dodge game. The player moves one circle with
a floating on-screen joystick and survives for as long as possible while five
kinds of hazard fly in from the screen edges: fast projectiles, ground area
attacks, fixed beams, homing orbs and returning boomerangs.

There are no levels and no waves. The spawn rate climbs continuously with elapsed
time, so every run is a single unbroken escalation and the score is simply how
long the player lasted.

Target audience is casual and arcade players aged 13 and over. The app is rated
4+ as it contains no violence, no realistic imagery and no text communication —
all visuals are abstract neon shapes drawn in code.

The value it provides is a short-session reflex challenge: a run lasts seconds to
a couple of minutes, and personal best times are kept on the device so the player
competes against themselves.


4. HOW TO ACCESS EVERY FEATURE
------------------------------
No account, login, password, subscription or sample file is required. Nothing in
the app is gated, purchasable or hidden behind any credential.

- Launch the app. On first launch iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  Either answer is accepted and gameplay is identical afterwards.
- The main menu shows a "Loadout" panel with three optional abilities: Flash,
  Dash and Ghost. Tap the cards to arm or disarm them. Running with none armed
  marks the score with a "purist" badge.
- Tap PLAY. A 3-2-1 countdown runs, then the game begins.
- Drag anywhere on the screen to move. A joystick appears wherever the finger
  lands and the circle follows it.
- Tap the round ability buttons (F, D, G) to use an armed ability. Each has its
  own cooldown, shown as a number inside the button.
- Touching any hazard ends the run.
- On the death screen: RETRY starts another run, MENU returns to the main menu,
  and "REVIVE — WATCH AD" offers a rewarded advert to continue the same run.
- "Customise controls" on the main menu opens a screen where the ability buttons
  can be dragged to any position and resized.
- Best times appear on the main menu and the death screen. They are stored only
  on the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.


5. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
-----------------------------------------
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) — advertising. Interstitial, rewarded
  video and rewarded interstitial formats.
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — collects advertising consent from users
  in the EEA and the UK, as Google requires before serving personalised ads.
- Apple App Tracking Transparency — the system tracking permission prompt.
- Capacitor (Ionic) — the native wrapper. The game itself is HTML5 and Canvas.

There are no servers operated by us. There is no authentication service, no
payment processor, no analytics platform of ours and no AI service. No data is
transmitted to any service we control.


6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
-----------------------
Gameplay, features and content are identical in every region.

The only difference is a legal one: users in the EEA and the UK are shown
Google's UMP consent form before personalised advertising is served, and a
"Privacy & ad settings" button is present on the main menu so they can change
that choice at any time. Outside those regions the form is not shown. Nothing
about the game itself changes.


7. REGULATED INDUSTRY OR PROTECTED MATERIAL
-------------------------------------------

Third-party material is limited to two typefaces, Oxanium and Barlow, both used

=======================================================
SCREEN RECORDING CHECKLIST
=======================================================
Record on the iPhone: swipe into Control Centre and tap the record button.
Apple requires it to start from launching the app.

  1. Start recording on the Home screen, then tap the Skillshot icon.
  2. Let the App Tracking Transparency prompt appear and answer it — Apple
     specifically asks to see this prompt.
  3. Show the main menu. Tap an ability card to arm and disarm it.
  4. Open "Customise controls", drag a button, resize it, tap DONE.
  5. Tap PLAY. Play a full run, using at least one ability, until you die.
  6. On the death screen show the buttons, including "REVIVE — WATCH AD".
  7. Play again until an advert appears, and let it play and close.
  8. Return to the menu so the best-times list is visible.

Two minutes is plenty. Upload it when replying to App Review.
