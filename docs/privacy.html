Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

Skillshot is a mobile game published by Ondrej Luka. This policy explains what the app collects and why. It is short, because the app does very little.

No account, no personal details

Skillshot has no sign-in. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number or location, and there is no server of ours storing anything about you.

Your scores stay on your device

Best times and leaderboard entries are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

Skillshot shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, and the approximate region derived from it
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • your interactions with ads shown in the app

We never see or store this data ourselves. Google handles it under its own policies:

Google Privacy Policy
How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices in the EEA and UK

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your answer at any time: open the app's main menu and tap Privacy & ad settings.

Resetting your advertising ID

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID whenever you like, under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised advertising.

Children

Skillshot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date above.
